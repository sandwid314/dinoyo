## Solving a Quadratic Equation

We can factor the equation:

\[
x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0
\]

The solutions are **x = 2** and **x = 3**.

![Graph of the quadratic equation](/assets/uploads/testimage2.png)

Here is a short Python example:

```python
import sympy as sp

x = sp.symbols("x")
sp.solve(x**2 - 5*x + 6, x)
```
